Delete your Glow account

You can delete your Glow account here, or in the app under Profile → Settings → Delete account.

What gets deleted:

  • Your name, email, phone number, salon details and profile photo
  • Saved delivery addresses and location
  • Your cart, favourites, reviews, recipes and notifications
  • Push notification tokens for your devices

What we keep: records of completed orders (order number, items, amounts and dates) for accounting and tax purposes, with your name, phone number and delivery address removed.

If you have an order in progress, please wait until it is delivered or cancel it before deleting your account. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Forgot your password or can't sign in? Contact Glow support and we will delete your account for you.
